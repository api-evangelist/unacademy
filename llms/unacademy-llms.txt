# Unacademy

> Unacademy is a Bangalore-based online learning platform — one of India's largest — offering live classes, courses, study material, and test preparation for competitive exams including UPSC Civil Services, IIT JEE, NEET-UG, SSC, CA Foundation, and CSIR UGC NET, through its web platform and learner, educator, and parent mobile apps.
